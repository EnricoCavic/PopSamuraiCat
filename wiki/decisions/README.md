# Decisions

**This folder owns *why it is that way*.** A page records a **fork**: what was chosen, what was
rejected, what the rejected option would have cost, and what would reopen the question. That last
part is what makes a decision page worth writing — six months on, the question always comes back,
and the page is what stops it being re-argued from scratch.

**A decision recorded here is locked.** It is not reopened casually and never silently reversed.
Changing one means a **new page superseding it**, with the old page left in place pointing at what
replaced it.

**Never contains** the resulting design restated, the code it lands on, or the work that shipped it.
Those are [[design/README|design]], [[systems/README|systems]] and [[tasks/README|tasks]]. Link to
them, with one clause of gloss and never the reasoning ([[SCHEMA]] § *The linking rule*).

**Usually attached to a task.** A decision can start a task, land in the middle of one, or fall out
of the end of one. Link the task from the decision and the decision from the task, and write the
substance once, in whichever of the two owns it.

**Numbering:** `DEC-NNNN-short-name.md`, sequential from `DEC-0001`, **never renumbered**. This
register is **this project's own**. The method that deployed this wiki keeps a decision register of
its own, and its numbers never appear here — a rule you disagree with is a finding about the method,
raised upstream, not a decision page in this repo.

**Empty.** No decision has been locked yet.

**Full register: [[index]] § *Decisions*.** It is the only catalog — this README explains the
folder, it does not list its contents.
