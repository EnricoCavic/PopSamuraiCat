# Design

**This folder owns *what the product is meant to be*** — stated as settled fact, in the present
tense. It grows two ways: a source lands in [[raw/README|raw]], or a design question gets settled
with the human.

**Never contains** the alternatives that were rejected, the argument that produced the design, or
when it was decided. A page here says what the thing *is*; [[decisions/README|decisions]] says why
it is that, and [[tasks/README|tasks]] says when it changed.

**Settling a question produces one of three outcomes**, and telling them apart is the discipline
this folder rests on ([[SCHEMA]] § *Design work is not ingest*):

- **A real fork** — an alternative was rejected and the reason is worth remembering. The design page
  states the result; a `decisions/` page records the fork and its cost.
- **Unstated detail** — a small specific the design had not pinned down. The design page only. No
  decision page: nothing was rejected, and a register of one-line non-choices stops being readable.
- **A question about what the code already does.** Not a design question at all — `/brain-os:query`
  it and update [[systems/README|systems]].

**Empty.** Nothing has been written here yet.

**Full register: [[index]] § *Design*.** It is the only catalog — this README explains the folder,
it does not list its contents.
